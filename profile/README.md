# Taverlo

## Know what your domain tells email providers.

Taverlo checks the public DMARC and SPF records for a business domain and explains what they mean in plain English.

[Check your domain free →](https://taverlo.com/dmarc-checker)

No account or card is needed for the public check. Taverlo does not read an inbox or change DNS. If you need to know when the answer changes, monitor one domain free; continued monitoring is $29/month for up to five domains, with no automatic conversion.
