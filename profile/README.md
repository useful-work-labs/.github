# Taverlo

## Know what your domain tells email providers.

Taverlo checks the public DMARC and SPF records for a business domain and explains what they mean in plain English.

[Check your domain free →](https://taverlo.com/dmarc-checker)

No account or card is needed for the public check. Taverlo does not read an inbox or change DNS. New monitoring accounts are temporarily unavailable.
